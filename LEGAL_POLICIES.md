## 2. Privacy Policy
### Data Collection and Usage
- Locator-X stores data *only locally* in your browser's storage (chrome.storage.local or similar)
- No data is transmitted to any third-party servers unless explicitly configured by you
- Data stored includes saved locators, POM pages, history, settings, and preferences
- No personal identification information (PII) is collected by default

### Third-Party Services
This extension may interact with websites you visit; use of those websites is subject to their respective privacy policies.

## 4. Security Notice
### Scope of Responsibility
- The Locator-X developers are responsible for secure coding practices in the extension itself
- Users are responsible for:
  - Securely using locators generated by the extension
  - Ensuring the websites they test are authorized for testing
  - Protecting any sensitive data stored in their browser

### Security Recommendations
- Keep your browser and extension updated to the latest version
- Only use the extension on websites you own or have explicit permission to test
- Do not store sensitive data (e.g., credentials) in saved locators
